# Ambrane Customer Support — Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

**Q: How do I register my Ambrane product warranty?**
A: Visit **ambraneindia.com/pages/product-registration** or **myproductcare.com** within 15 days of purchase. You'll need: product model, serial number, purchase date, invoice upload, and purchase channel (Amazon, Flipkart, Ambrane website, etc.).

**Q: Where can I find the serial number?**
A: The serial number is printed on the product box barcode label and on the back or bottom of the product itself. It usually starts with "AMB" or is a 12-to-16 digit numeric/alphanumeric code.

**Q: What is the support helpline and timing?**
A: You can reach us via phone at **+91-11-69951111** (Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM) or via email at **care@ambraneindia.com**. WhatsApp chat option is also available on our website.

## Order & Shipping Related

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: Once shipped, you'll receive a tracking link via SMS and email. You can track your package using the tracking number (AWB) directly on the courier partner website or contact support.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after ordering?**
A: If the order has not been shipped yet, you can contact support immediately to update the address. Once shipped, address redirection is not possible.

**Q: What is the cancellation policy?**
A: Orders can be cancelled before shipment for a full refund as a store gift card. If cancelled after shipment (within 48 hours of ordering), a ₹50 shipping charge is deducted, and the rest is refunded as a gift card.

## Smartwatches Troubleshooting

**Q: Which companion app should I download for my Ambrane smartwatch?**
A: Ambrane smartwatches use different companion apps depending on the model. Ensure you download the correct app specified in your user manual:
- **Da Fit**: Used for Wise Eon, Wise Eon Pro, and Wise Eon Max.
- **GloryFit**: Used for Wise Rush, Wise Roam, and FitShot Sphere.
- **HaWoFit**: Used for Wise Glaze.
- **FitCloudPro**: Used for Wise Crest, Wise Eon Luxe, and Wise Glaze 2.

**Q: My smartwatch is not pairing with my phone?**
A: 1) Keep the watch and phone close. 2) Turn on Bluetooth and Location services on your phone. 3) Do NOT pair directly via the phone's Bluetooth settings; instead, open the companion app and add the device inside the app. 4) If it fails, restart both phone and watch, clear the companion app cache, and retry.

**Q: How do I set up Bluetooth Calling on my Ambrane watch?**
A: 
- **Android**: Pair the watch inside the companion app first. A prompt for "Phone Audio / Calling" will automatically pop up. Accept it.
- **iOS**: Pair the watch inside the companion app first. Then go to iPhone **Settings > Bluetooth**, scan for the secondary Bluetooth name (usually ends with "Phone" or "Call"), and tap to connect.

## Power Banks Troubleshooting

**Q: My power bank is not charging?**
A: 1) Try a different charging cable (high-quality Type-C cable is recommended). 2) Make sure the wall adapter is at least 10W (preferably 18W/20W/22.5W QC/PD fast charger). 3) Connect the power bank and charge it continuously for 4-5 hours. 4) If the LED lights do not blink at all, try resetting the power bank by holding the power button for 10-15 seconds.

**Q: Can I carry my Ambrane power bank on a flight?**
A: Yes, power banks up to **100Wh (approx. 27,000mAh)** are allowed in cabin baggage (carry-on). They are strictly prohibited in checked-in luggage.

## Audio Support (TWS Earbuds & Neckbands)

**Q: My TWS earbuds are not pairing or only one side is working?**
A: This is usually due to a synchronization error. Reset them: 1) Forget/Delete "Ambrane" from Bluetooth settings. 2) Put both earbuds inside the charging case. 3) Take both earbuds out at the exact same time. They will flash lights and pair with each other. 4) Turn on Bluetooth on your phone and connect.

**Q: How do I reset my Ambrane neckband?**
A: Turn off the neckband. Press and hold the Volume Up (+) and Volume Down (-) buttons simultaneously for 5 seconds until the LED light flashes red and blue. The neckband is now reset and ready to pair.
