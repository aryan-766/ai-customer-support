# Ambrane Customer Support — Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

**Q: How do I register my Ambrane product?**
A: Visit myproductcare.com or call 1800-XXX-XXXX. You'll need: product model, serial number, purchase date, and purchase channel (Amazon, Flipkart, Ambrane website, etc.).

**Q: Where can I find the serial number?**
A: The serial number is printed on the product box and on the back/bottom of the product. It usually starts with "AMB" followed by digits.

**Q: What is the warranty period?**
A: All Ambrane products come with 1-year standard warranty from purchase date. Extended 2-year warranty available via My Product Care.

## Order Related

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: You'll receive a tracking link via SMS/email after shipment. Alternatively, call support with your order ID.

**Q: My order shows delivered but I haven't received it?**
A: Please check with neighbors and your building security first. If still not found, contact us within 48 hours of delivery status with your order ID.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after ordering?**
A: Address can be changed before shipment. Once shipped, it cannot be changed. Contact us immediately if needed.

## Product Support

**Q: My Bluetooth earphones are not connecting?**
A: 1) Turn off Bluetooth on phone. 2) Put earphones back in case for 10 seconds. 3) Remove from case, hold button for 5 seconds (red-blue flash = pairing mode). 4) Turn on Bluetooth and connect.

**Q: My power bank is not charging?**
A: 1) Try a different cable (original USB-C preferred). 2) Ensure wall adapter supports at least 10W. 3) Check if charging indicator lights blink. 4) Try charging for 30 minutes before connecting devices.

**Q: My neckband audio quality is poor?**
A: 1) Clean earbuds with dry cloth. 2) Check if EQ setting on phone is misconfigured. 3) Ensure neckband is within 10 meters of connected device. 4) Re-pair the device fresh.

## Invoice & Billing

**Q: How do I get a GST invoice?**
A: Call support or use the Ambrane app. Provide your GSTIN number. Invoice will be emailed within 24 hours.

**Q: Can I get a duplicate invoice?**
A: Yes. Share your order ID or registered mobile number. Invoice will be sent to your registered email.
