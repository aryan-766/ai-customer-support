# Ambrane Power Bank Troubleshooting Manual

This manual provides instructions for diagnosing and resolving charging issues with Ambrane power banks.

## 1. Power Bank Failing to Charge (Input Issue)
If the power bank's LED indicators do not blink or light up when connected to a charger:
*   **Step 1: Inspect Cable & Charger**: Try a different USB-C or Micro-USB cable. Ensure the wall charger is fully operational and rated for at least 10W (preferably 18W+ Quick Charge/Power Delivery charger).
*   **Step 2: Check Charging Indicator**:
    *   **Blinking Light**: Indicates charging is in progress.
    *   **Solid Lights**: Indicates battery level (each light is ~25%).
    *   **No Lights**: Power bank is not receiving power or is fully discharged.
*   **Step 3: Power Reset**: Hold down the power bank's side power button for 10-15 seconds. This resets the internal circuit board controller.
*   **Step 4: Continuous Charge**: Leave it plugged into a confirmed working charger for at least 4 hours. Deeply drained cells may take time to reactivate.

## 2. Power Bank Failing to Charge Devices (Output Issue)
If the power bank is fully charged but does not charge connected devices:
*   Ensure the device is connected to the correct output port (USB-A or USB-C).
*   Press the power bank's power button once to initiate charging.
*   Disconnect and reconnect the cable.
*   If using low-current devices (like smart bands or TWS earbuds), verify if the power bank has a low-current charging mode (double-press the power button to activate if supported).

## 3. Flight & Cabin Regulations
*   All power banks up to **20,000mAh (74Wh)** and **27,000mAh (99.9Wh)** are allowed in cabin baggage.
*   Power banks are strictly **prohibited** in checked-in (cargo) luggage due to aviation safety regulations.
*   Any power bank above 100Wh requires prior airline approval.
