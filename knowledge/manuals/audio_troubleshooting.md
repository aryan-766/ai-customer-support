# Ambrane Audio (TWS & Neckbands) Troubleshooting Manual

This manual provides instructions for resolving connection, pairing, and audio quality issues with Ambrane TWS earbuds and Bluetooth neckbands.

## 1. TWS Earbuds Pairing Synchronization (One Side Not Working)
When only one earbud has audio, it means the two earbuds have desynchronized from each other.
*   **Reset & Re-sync Process**:
    1. **Delete Pairing**: Go to smartphone Bluetooth settings and forget/delete all entries for "Ambrane". Turn off Bluetooth.
    2. **Power Off**: Keep both earbuds inside the charging case. Ensure they show a red charging light.
    3. **Clear Memory / Reset**: Take both earbuds out. Press and hold the touch sensor on both earbuds simultaneously for 8-10 seconds until the LED flashes red/blue or turns off. Put them back in the case for 10 seconds.
    4. **Simultaneous Power On**: Remove both earbuds from the case at the exact same time. They will flash lights and automatically pair with each other (indicated by a sound prompt or one earbud light turning off).
    5. **Connect**: Enable Bluetooth on the smartphone, search, and connect to the single "Ambrane" entry.

## 2. Bluetooth Neckband Reset
For neckbands experiencing connectivity lag, low volume, or pairing failures:
*   **Step 1**: Turn off the neckband.
*   **Step 2**: Press and hold the **Volume Up (+)** and **Volume Down (-)** buttons together for 5-7 seconds.
*   **Step 3**: The LED indicator will flash Red and Blue three times, indicating the reset is complete.
*   **Step 4**: Turn Bluetooth on your phone off and on, scan, and re-pair.

## 3. Audio Quality & Charging Issues
*   **Low Volume / Muffled Sound**: Clean the earbud mesh gently using a soft, dry cotton swab to remove earwax or dust.
*   **Fails to Charge**: Ensure the plastic protective sticker on the charging contacts of new TWS earbuds has been removed. Wipe the metal contacts on the earbuds and inside the charging case with dry cotton.
